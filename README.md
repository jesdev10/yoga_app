# Bliss Mind

A wellness and session-booking platform that makes it easy for clients
to discover wellness services, book sessions, and receive booking
confirmations.

## About the Project

Bliss Mind is a wellness platform designed to connect clients with
wellness services such as:

- Yoga
- Pilates
- Meditation
- Ice Bath Sessions

The platform allows clients to view available sessions, make bookings,
and receive confirmation after a successful booking.

It also provides a portal for instructors to submit their CVs and
manage their participation in the wellness program.

## Problem

Booking wellness sessions can be difficult when clients have to rely
on manual communication or separate channels to find available
sessions and arrange appointments.

Bliss Mind provides a centralized platform where clients can discover
services and manage their bookings.

## Key Features

- Browse available wellness sessions
- Book a wellness session
- Booking confirmation notifications
- Support for Yoga, Pilates, Meditation and Ice Bath sessions
- Session duration management
- Instructor CV submission
- Wellness articles and content
- Flexible payment options

## Target Users

- Customers
- Wellness instructors
- Administrators
